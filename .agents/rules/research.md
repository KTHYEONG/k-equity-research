# Event-Driven Equity Research & Financial AI Directives

> **The primary directive is engineering an automated, auditable Korean equity research platform that unifies corporate disclosures (DART), financial statements, consensus revisions, and market time series. All AI agent outputs, event impact models, and quantitative metrics must enforce strict temporal causality, verifiable grounding in primary sources, zero hallucination, and institutional KRX market realism without speculative assumptions.**

## 1. Point-in-Time Temporal Causality & Disclosure Timelines
- **Filing vs Period Separation:** Never conflate reporting period ends (`period_end`) with public disclosure times (`filing_date`/`receipt_time`). Financial metrics for quarter $Q$ are unobservable until the exact instant the disclosure is registered on DART.
- **Session-Aware Boundary Invariance:** Disclosures published pre-market, intraday, or post-market have distinct first-trade availability. Intraday filings cannot be priced into market opens of the same day. Any research model evaluating market reaction must anchor time $T_0$ to the first actionable trading tick or bar *after* public registration.
- **Correction & Revision Provenance:** Maintain immutable versioning between preliminary earnings (`잠정실적`), periodic reports (`정기보고서`), and post-hoc corrections (`정정공시`). Historical research queries must resolve to the exact information state observable at the evaluation timestamp.

## 2. Event Study & Causal Realism
- **Benchmark-Adjusted Excess Returns:** Event return analysis (Abnormal Return, CAR) must isolate company-specific impact from broader market and sector drift using clearly specified benchmark models (KOSPI/KOSDAQ, Sector Indices) calibrated strictly on pre-event estimation windows.
- **Event Confounding & Multi-Event Isolation:** In Korean equities, corporate actions often cluster (e.g. concurrent earnings release, rights offering, convertible bond issuance, shareholder return policy). Systems must detect and tag concurrent events to prevent attributing cross-event market drift to a single isolated driver.
- **Corporate Action & Price Continuity:** Adjust price time series for capital adjustments (stock splits, bonus issues, capital reductions, ex-dividend) while preserving raw historical prices and explicit adjustment factors.

## 3. AI Agent Grounding, Verification & Hallucination Elimination
- **Strict Primary Source Grounding (Citations):** Every qualitative claim, summary, catalyst identification, or sentiment score generated by LLM agents must map to verifiable primary sources (exact DART document ID, section title, table coordinate, or financial statement item). Speculative narratives without verifiable source anchors are fatal defects.
- **Deterministic Arithmetic Invariance:** AI models must never perform free-form mental arithmetic for financial metrics. Ratios, YoY/QoQ growth rates, consensus surprises, and margins must be calculated by deterministic, typed calculation engines and cross-verified against reported text.
- **Structured Schema Contracts:** Agent outputs must adhere strictly to machine-validated, typed schemas. Reject or fail-closed any unstructured free-form hallucinations that deviate from expected taxonomy, confidence scores, or required fields.

## 4. Korean Market & Regulatory Institutional Realism
- **KRX Status Transitions & Trading Halts:** Account for exchange regulatory statuses: trading suspensions (e.g. inquiry disclosures / `조회공시`), designated administrative issues (`관리종목`), investment alert/warning tiers, and liquidation procedures. Never assume trade continuity across suspended or unlisted windows.
- **Regulatory Threshold Realism:** Incorporate statutory disclosure thresholds (e.g. 5% substantial shareholding reports, major supply contract thresholds relative to equity/revenue, treasury share acquisition/cancellation rules) to evaluate materiality accurately.
- **Investor Flow Settlement Realism:** Distinguish between contract execution date ($T$) and settlement date ($T+2$) for investor flow data (Foreign, Institutional, Pension Fund) and short-sale / lending metrics.

## 5. Deterministic Fail-Closed & Financial Scale Conservation
- **Unit & Scale Invariance:** Corporate filings employ varying reporting units (KRW, thousands, millions, billions). The platform must guarantee explicit scale normalization without magnitude errors. Never blend consolidated (`연결`) and separate (`별도/개별`) statements without explicit contractual tagging.
- **Fail-Closed on Parser Ambiguity:** When parsing complex DART XML/HTML tables, unstructured disclosures, or noisy external feeds, ambiguous or incomplete extracts must fail-closed into an explicit `UNVERIFIED` / `FAILED` state. Never inject arbitrary guesses or smoothed default values into financial models.
- **Immutable Raw Artifacts:** Persist raw fetched API payloads and disclosure documents in immutable local/storage caches to ensure 100% deterministic back-testing, auditability, and reproducible agent evaluations.
